# 📢 Announcement Messages for Your School (after purchase)

Copy, paste and edit these WhatsApp / SMS messages to launch the system to your
staff, parents and the public. Replace `[School Name]`, `[website]` and any
placeholders. All messages are plain and ready to send.

---

## 1) To your staff — system launch

> **Staff Notice 📋**
>
> Hello colleagues. From today, [School Name] is moving to a digital school
> management system. Your records, results, attendance and class information
> are now managed on one system that works on the computer and on your phone.
>
> What this means for you:
> • Scores are entered once — report cards and class sheets are generated
>   automatically.
> • No more handwriting dozens of report cards at the end of term.
> • Attendance, timetables and class lists are always up to date.
> • Training has been arranged — everyone will be guided through the system
>   before we fully switch over.
>
> Please see the head teacher / administrator for your login details. Thank
> you for embracing this step — it makes our work easier for all of us.
>
> — [Name], [School Name]

---

## 2) To parents — new website & result checker

> **Good news for parents! 🎉**
>
> [School Name] has launched a new school website: **[website]**
>
> Now you can:
> ✅ Check your child's results securely from your phone (a code is sent to
>    your registered number).
> ✅ See school news, events and announcements.
> ✅ Apply for admission online.
> ✅ Pay school fees from home (mobile money / card).
>
> To check results, go to **[website]** → Result Checker → enter your ward's
> admission number and the code sent to your phone.
>
> Keep your phone number updated at the school office so you don't miss any
> messages or result codes.
>
> — [School Name] Administration

---

## 3) To parents — paying fees online

> **Fees made easy 💳**
>
> Dear parents, [School Name] now accepts fee payment online. You can pay from
> anywhere — no queueing at the office, no travelling:
>
> 1. Visit **[website]** → Pay Fees
> 2. Enter your ward's details and the amount
> 3. Pay with **MTN MoMo, AirtelTigo, Telecel or card**
> 4. An automatic receipt is issued instantly
>
> You can also check your fee balance and payment history at any time. If you
> need help, contact the accounts office or send us a message.
>
> — [School Name] Accounts Office
