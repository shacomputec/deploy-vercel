### What is included (one purchase)

1. **School website** — your name, logo and colours: home, about, programmes,
   news & events, gallery, admissions and contact.
2. **Management system** — students, staff, teachers, classes & subjects;
   school-based assessments; automatic report cards, class & master sheets;
   timetables; attendance; fees, receipts & arrears; expenses & payroll;
   library, hostel, transport, sick bay, discipline, clubs & inventory.
3. **Android & Windows apps** — staff can work on computer or phone; all
   sharing one live database, with offline mode.
4. **Public services** — secure result checker for parents (SMS code),
   online admissions, online fee payment (MTN MoMo, AirtelTigo, Telecel,
   Paystack — to the school's own accounts) and a messaging centre
   (SMS / WhatsApp / email).
5. **Setup & training** — installation, staff training session, in-app guide.
6. **Support & updates** — direct support from the developer; updates included.

**Follows the GES/NaCCA curriculum — Crèche to Senior High.**
